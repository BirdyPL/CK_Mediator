## 8. Selling-language notes (for translator + me)

When you translate or refine, keep these PDF-derived rules visible:

1. **Speak ROI, not awareness.** Wherever possible, frame copy as outcome ("first sales by Day 90", "category share gains") not vibes ("brand exposure", "presence").

2. **Hammer locality.** Repeat *"Polish VAT invoice, Warsaw service center, 24h delivery"* across multiple screens — this is the trust signal that opens Chinese MDF budgets.

3. **Co-op as trust gesture.** Mention "we share media spend 50/50" prominently in Gold/Platinum — partnership signal, not vendor-only spend.

4. **Menu, not co-creation.** Silver/Gold/Platinum framed as ready-to-pick. Avoid "let's design together" phrasing — Chinese decision makers prefer choosing from validated templates.

5. **No hedging.** Cut "we could", "potentially", "depending on" — replace with concrete commitments ("we deliver", "by day 30", "you get").
